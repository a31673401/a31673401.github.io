Research Interests

讓生理監測「可靠地」察覺異常Making physiological monitoring detect anomalies — reliably

我的研究興趣是資源受限裝置上的生理訊號分析：在微控制器等級的硬體上，即時判斷心電／PPG 訊號是否可信，並以輕量化模型完成異常偵測，降低假警報。

I am interested in physiological signal analysis on resource-constrained devices: judging in real time whether ECG/PPG signals can be trusted on MCU-class hardware, and detecting anomalies with lightweight models to reduce false alarms.

起點Where it started

做多床位版本的智慧看護系統時，系統常因病人翻身或電極鬆脫而誤發警報。這讓我意識到：把感測器接起來並不難，難的是判斷「這筆資料可不可信」、「這個異常是不是真的」。在醫院中，大量假警報會造成「警報疲乏」，反而讓真正的異常被忽略。

While building the multi-bed system, motion and loose electrodes kept triggering false alarms. Connecting sensors is easy; the hard part is knowing whether the data can be trusted and whether an anomaly is real. In hospitals, too many false alarms lead to alarm fatigue.

想回答的問題Questions

Q1 · 訊號可信度Signal quality

如何在有限的運算資源下，即時評估 ECG／PPG 的訊號品質，並在品質不足時避免錯誤判斷？

How can we assess ECG/PPG quality in real time with limited compute, and avoid wrong decisions when quality is poor?

Q2 · 輕量化辨識Lightweight models

如何讓心律異常辨識模型在微控制器上執行，同時維持可接受的準確率？

How can arrhythmia models run on microcontrollers while keeping acceptable accuracy?

Q3 · 多模態融合Multimodal fusion

如何結合多種生理訊號與其品質資訊，在降低假警報的同時，維持對真實事件的靈敏度？

How can multiple signals and their quality be fused to cut false alarms while staying sensitive to real events?

規劃的方法Planned approach

  1. 資料與基準：使用 MIT-BIH、PhysioNet/CinC 2017、PPG-DaLiA 等公開資料庫，先以 Pan-Tompkins 等傳統方法建立基準。
  2. Data & baselines: public datasets (MIT-BIH, PhysioNet/CinC 2017, PPG-DaLiA) with classical baselines such as Pan-Tompkins.
  3. 訊號品質指標：設計可在 MCU 上即時計算的指標，例如模板相關性、頻譜能量比、ECG 與 PPG 心率一致性。
  4. Quality indices: MCU-friendly indices such as template correlation, spectral energy ratio and ECG–PPG heart-rate agreement.
  5. 輕量化模型：以一維 CNN 為主，透過 8 位元整數量化等方法壓縮，評估模型大小、記憶體與延遲。
  6. Lightweight models: 1-D CNNs compressed with 8-bit integer quantization; measure size, memory and latency.
  7. 融合與警報決策：以品質指標作為權重融合多種訊號的判斷，並與固定門檻法比較。
  8. Fusion & alarms: quality-weighted fusion compared against fixed thresholds.
  9. 系統驗證：部署在智慧看護平台，以靈敏度、F1、每小時假警報次數、延遲與功耗評估。
  10. Validation: deploy on the smart-nursing platform; evaluate sensitivity, F1, false alarms per hour, latency and power.
