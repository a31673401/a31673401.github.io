關於我About

我是張存瑀，2026 年 6 月畢業於南臺科技大學電機工程系控制與晶片組。大學期間參與多項實作專題與競賽，從機械手臂、移動載具到生理監測系統都親手做過。

投入最深的是大學專題「智慧看護系統」：在開發多床位版本時，我發現系統常因病人翻身或電極鬆脫而誤發警報──資料「有收到」不代表「可以相信」。這個問題讓我開始研讀訊號品質評估與輕量化模型的文獻，也決定繼續攻讀碩士。

I graduated from the Department of Electrical Engineering (Control & IC Design track), STUST, in June 2026. During college I built robotic arms, mobile vehicles and physiological monitoring systems through hands-on projects and competitions.

My main project is a Smart Nursing Monitoring System. While extending it to multiple beds, I found that false alarms caused by motion or loose electrodes are common — receiving data does not mean the data can be trusted. This question led me to study signal quality assessment and lightweight models, and to pursue a master's degree.

智慧看護系統 → 多床位集中監控系統Smart Nursing System → Multi-Bed Centralized Monitoring

以微控制器整合心電（AD8232）、血氧心率（MAX30102）、體溫與點滴液位／重量感測，透過藍牙即時傳送至手機 App，並在異常時發出警示；之後擴充為多床位版本，由各床感測節點將資料彙整至 Raspberry Pi 5，再以 App 與網頁儀表板集中監控。

Integrates ECG (AD8232), SpO₂/heart rate (MAX30102), body temperature and IV drip level/weight sensing on a microcontroller, streams data to a mobile app over BLE and raises alerts. Later extended to a multi-bed version: sensor nodes per bed → Raspberry Pi 5 → app and web dashboard.

BMduino / HT32 sensor nodes → Raspberry Pi 5 (PostgreSQL) → Flutter App · Web Dashboard

我的負責：App 與資料庫開發（Flutter、BLE 資料接收、SQLite、警報通知）；盛群盃版本中除硬體外之軟體皆由我負責。

My role: app & database development (Flutter, BLE, SQLite, alert notifications); all non-hardware software for the Holtek Cup version.

FlutterBLESQLitePostgreSQLRaspberry Pi 5HT32
校內競賽第三名3rd Place · Dept. Contest

智能護伴：視覺載具與機械手臂Smart Companion: Vision Vehicle & Robotic Arm

結合視覺載具與機械手臂的照護輔助作品。我負責機械手臂的藥品抓取，為了讓手臂準確到位而補強正逆運動學，並針對載具停止位置的偏差，依實際位置即時調整手臂姿態。

A care-assist robot combining a vision vehicle and a robotic arm. I handled medicine grasping with the arm, applying forward/inverse kinematics and compensating for the vehicle's stopping-position error in real time.

KinematicsRobotic ArmComputer Vision

以移動載具搭配影像偵測的監測平台。我負責車輛硬體製作：壓克力車體的材料切割、鑽孔與組裝，四馬達驅動，讓團隊的影像與控制程式能在實體載具上測試與展示。

A monitoring platform built on a mobile vehicle with image detection. I built the vehicle hardware — cutting, drilling and assembling the acrylic chassis with a four-motor drive.
