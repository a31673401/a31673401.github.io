視覺載具 × 機械手臂的照護輔助作品A care-assist robot: vision vehicle × robotic arm

教育部新工程教育方法實驗與建構計畫「視覺輔助載具實習」的課程作品，結合會移動的視覺載具與機械手臂，模擬在照護場景中協助取藥。作品獲得電機系校內競賽（視覺載具）第三名。

A project from the MOE New Engineering Education program (Vision-Assisted Vehicle Lab), combining a mobile vision vehicle and a robotic arm to assist with fetching medicine. It won 3rd place in the department contest.

Robotic ArmForward / Inverse KinematicsVision Vehicle
我的負責My role

機械手臂的藥品抓取：讓手臂在載具停下後，準確移動到藥品位置並完成抓取。

Medicine grasping with the robotic arm: moving the arm precisely to the medicine and picking it up after the vehicle stops.

過程How it went

  1. 補強運動學：寫手臂控制時發現，要讓夾爪準確到達目標，必須理解正運動學（關節角度 → 夾爪位置）與逆運動學（目標位置 → 關節角度），因此自行補強了這部分的理論。
  2. Learning kinematics: to reach targets precisely I studied forward kinematics (joint angles → gripper pose) and inverse kinematics (target → joint angles).
  3. 遇到的問題：載具每次停下來的位置都會有些偏差，手臂如果照固定角度動作就會抓空。
  4. The problem: the vehicle never stopped at exactly the same spot, so fixed arm motions missed the target.
  5. 解法：依載具實際停下的位置，即時調整手臂的目標姿態，而不是只跑固定的動作。
  6. The fix: adjust the arm's target pose in real time based on where the vehicle actually stopped.
學到的事Takeaway

這次讓我體會到理論模型與實際系統之間的落差，控制不能只假設一切理想，還要根據實際情況修正。

It showed me the gap between theoretical models and real systems — control has to correct for what actually happens, not just assume ideal conditions.
