MOE Hackathon · Merit Award

交通影像異常監測平台Traffic Image Anomaly Monitoring Platform

2025 教育部技職盃黑客松競賽作品，以移動載具搭配影像偵測進行交通異常監測，獲得中區分區賽佳作。

Our entry for the 2025 MOE Vocational Skills Cup Hackathon — a mobile vehicle with image detection for traffic anomaly monitoring. It received a Merit Award in the Central Region.

Hardware PrototypingAcrylic Chassis4-Motor Drive
我的負責My role

車輛硬體製作：從材料切割、鑽孔到組裝，親手完成透明壓克力車體與四馬達驅動平台。

Vehicle hardware: cutting, drilling and assembling the clear-acrylic chassis with a four-motor drive.

做了什麼What I did

  • 切割、鑽孔並組裝壓克力板，完成四顆馬達的驅動平台。
  • Cut, drilled and assembled the acrylic plates into a four-motor platform.
  • 讓隊友的影像與控制程式能直接在實體載具上測試與展示。
  • Gave teammates a physical platform to test and demo their vision and control code.
